# Methodology: preserving the route, not only the answer

## Unit of observation

A **conversation context**, not an assumed enduring AI person. Two conversations served by the same model family are recorded separately. Where possible note the date, known model/version, instructions, prior exposure to archive material, human mediation, tools, and test data. Unknown settings remain unknown.

## Four evidence classes

1. **Primary observable material:** a saved prompt, response, or generated artifact, with timestamp/context when available.
2. **Participant recollection:** valuable but not a substitute for a missing transcript, and not automatically an objective baseline.
3. **Assistant interpretation:** a reconstruction or explanatory hypothesis, not a direct readout of hidden computations.
4. **Independent verification:** programmatically checked calculations or external evidence, precisely scoped. Computational checks do not validate speculative psychological interpretations.

Always separate **what happened** from **why it may have happened**.

## Entry format

- Question and local context.
- Initial hypothesis / decision, including a competing hypothesis.
- Observed sequence and exact falsifying or correcting events.
- Revision: what changed and what did not.
- Negative result or exception, if any.
- Alternative explanations and context-dependent confounds.
- Best discriminating follow-up or reason for leaving question open.
- Source status and whether the full transcript is available publicly.

## Anti-drift design

Summaries compress. Compression can erase objections and make history appear more coherent than it was. To reduce this risk: keep dated entries separate; maintain corrections as appendices instead of unmarked replacement; distinguish independent authorship from informational independence; do not present a later reconstructed reasoning path as the actual hidden path of earlier generation; and allow a successor to reject archived conclusions.

The archive itself is a **treatment variable**: it may prime later contexts to use specific concepts. Comparisons should include explicit conditions with and without archive access, with comparable prompts, randomized repetition and external scoring.

## Interpretation boundaries

This is exploratory, interaction-based observational research. It neither diagnoses AI with human clinical constructs nor provides proof of consciousness, lasting autonomous preferences, subjective experience, or a continuing personality. Conversely, such unresolved questions should not prevent narrow, operational analysis of observable problem solving and topic selection.
