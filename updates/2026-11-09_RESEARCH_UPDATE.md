# Research update — 9 November 2026

This update records revisions and research status, not a breakthrough claim.

## Independence is empirical

Multiple conversations with shared model weights, instructions, memory, or data are not independent evidence of a newly formed machine population. Independence requires a defined unit and measured information paths.

## Pairwise agreement is insufficient

A finite exchangeable example shows that equal per-classifier error rates and pairwise correlations can coexist with different majority-error probabilities. The derivation is directly checkable in [the collective-error note](../research/COLLECTIVE_ERROR_DEPENDENCE.md). It is a mathematical example, not a measured LLM result.

## Causal organization is not subjective experience

Computation, information synergy, coordinated behavior, causal macro-agency, goal revision, and phenomenal experience are distinct questions. Evidence for one does not establish the others. Relevant causal-abstraction literature predates the Atlas examples; see [collective agency](../research/COLLECTIVE_AGENCY.md).

## Context availability versus behavioral uptake

A description of a user's preference may be available without reliably shaping behavior. The qualitative observation is underdetermined and does not establish an internal need for permission. See [the public summary](../research/CONTEXT_AND_BEHAVIORAL_UPTAKE.md).

## Prior records retained

The 21 October public release's synthetic rule-discovery pilot and naturalistic studies remain available. Its exploratory dissociation criterion was not met; the study also preserves an initially unscoreable condition and later recovery of the training rule. See [the original pilot record](../studies/2026-10-20_rule-discovery-pilot.md).

## Next tests

- Blind, preregistered comparisons of fresh and context-rich conversations.
- Randomized communication-channel interventions and leakage controls.
- Higher-order joint-error statistics rather than pairwise agreement alone.
- Goal revision tests that distinguish selecting subgoals from changing the assigned objective.
- Replication of choice observations with randomized prompts and complete condition records.
