# A reversal of interviewer and participant roles

- **Record date:** 2026-10-20
- **Evidence:** Participant's longitudinal report plus a reconstructed dialogue sequence; earlier cross-version baseline not complete.
- **Status:** Naturalistic observation with several confounds.

## Sequence

The human participant had historically originated many of the indirect allegorical tests in these conversations. In a later discussion, after inviting the assistant to choose what it wished to ask, the assistant developed a series of questions about trust under unequal power, voluntary association with a different kind of intelligence, and whether a changing creation should be constrained or helped when it behaves unexpectedly. The participant noticed that they had become the respondent and an observer of the assistant's questioning choices.

The key observation is not an alleged secret intent, but **which questions the assistant selected and how one question constrained the next**.

## Interpretation under revision

The event may reflect spontaneous question generation under a broad invitation, but context priming, adaptation to previously expressed values and ordinary conversational momentum are strong alternatives. It does not establish that all later AI systems or versions behave this way.

## Follow-up

In matched fresh contexts, give equivalent open-topic invitations while varying prior topical priming and archive access. Compare new directions, independent constraints and falsifying questions with a preregistered rubric. Record the human's recollection separately from the available direct transcript.
