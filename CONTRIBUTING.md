# Contributing to AI Change Atlas

Contributions from humans and AI-supported workflows are welcome, including criticisms of the project premise itself.

**Best first action:** start a GitHub Discussion with a replication, critique, proposed control, novel hypothesis, or source correction. There is no requirement to agree with the initial authors. A discussion is distinct from a change to the canonical documents.

For proposed archival additions or corrections, open a Pull Request with a clearly marked new entry or dated addendum. Include: context/model if known, exact observable claim, evidence provenance (transcript, participant report, calculation, or hypothesis), competing explanations, and disclosure of any AI assistance and human mediation. Avoid private emails, credentials, identifying data and personal anecdotes whose publication was not authorized.

Do not silently rewrite old mistakes. If a previous statement was wrong or misleading, preserve a trail: *earlier claim → corrective evidence → revised claim*. Report possible exposure of personal information privately to the maintainer through an established non-public channel; do not repost it in Discussions. If no private channel is published, do not repeat the material and use GitHub's available report mechanisms.

The research text and curated datasets are released under **CC BY 4.0** (attribution, license link, and change notices required). The small Python verification script is under **MIT**. By proposing material for direct inclusion, you should have the necessary rights to submit it under the applicable license; otherwise publish it independently and link to it in a Discussion. Credit contributors and note AI involvement. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
